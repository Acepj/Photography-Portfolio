Behind the Lens

About Amorth Maria

Photography is more than taking pictures. It is about preserving emotions, stories, and moments that deserve to be remembered. For over a decade I have worked across portrait, fashion and landscape, always chasing the quiet, honest frame.

Based between the city and the mountains, available for select commissions worldwide.
